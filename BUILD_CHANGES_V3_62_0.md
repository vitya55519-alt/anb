# V3.62.0 — 10 levels, two paths

- Levels 1–3 are shared (Знакомство, Симпатия, Искра). At level 3 she asks
  «что я для тебя?» in the app chat with two answers: ❤️ romance or 🔥 passion
  (per girl, chosen once; also tilts path_axis and the premium manner).
- Levels 4–10 have their own names, perks, chat tone and level-up scenes on
  each path. A married girl / one with a boyfriend colours the path: romance
  becomes a secret love that ends with «я ушла от него» at level 10 (status
  becomes divorced), passion becomes a secret affair.
- New stages `voyage` (9) and `eternal` (10): same score gates as 8, more
  time together (continuity/connection 65 and 75). Premium-only like 7–8.
- Level panel: path badge, the fork buttons if pending, and a road map with
  both paths — the other one visible but locked («🔒 на пути романтики»).
- Texts and test mode updated from 1–8 to 1–10.
