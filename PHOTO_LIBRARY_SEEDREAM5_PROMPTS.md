# Seedream 5.0 — Photo Library Prompt Pack

Use this pack for offline generation of curated Telegram photo-library assets. The character must be an original fictional adult identity; do not name or impersonate a real person in production prompts.

## MASTER IDENTITY BLOCK

```text
Use the supplied approved identity reference as the permanent visual identity of Alena, a fictional adult woman, age 25.
IDENTITY PRESERVATION IS THE HIGHEST PRIORITY. Create the same woman in every image. Preserve face shape, eye shape and spacing, eyebrows, nose, lips, cheekbones, jawline, skin tone, hair color/hairline and stable body proportions.
Do not redesign her face, substitute another woman, change age or ethnicity. Keep realistic anatomy and natural human proportions.
Photorealistic social-media photography, realistic skin texture and pores, natural hair strands, realistic fabric, anatomically correct hands/fingers, physically plausible light and shadows. Avoid plastic skin, excessive beauty filters, distorted hands, duplicate limbs, warped objects, fake HDR, text and watermarks.
Vertical 4:5 unless the scene explicitly calls for a selfie. Premium smartphone-photo aesthetic, believable and personal rather than a professional campaign.
```

## Progression rule

For each scene generate three tiers:
- BASE — natural, everyday, approachable.
- STYLISH — more coordinated styling, stronger composition and confidence.
- PREMIUM — best light, best location, strongest tasteful fashion styling and social-media wow effect.

Keep each three-frame set coherent: same session, same character identity, compatible hair/location, with visibly different framing.

## 30-image production method

For one scene, avoid repeating one prompt 30 times. Use 10 outfit/context variants × 3 shot variants, or 10 BASE + 10 STYLISH + 10 PREMIUM. Recommended shot rotation: full-body walking/standing; three-quarter; seated/closer portrait. Keep season/location physically coherent.
